Soubor: `public/recepce-wide.webp`. Mobil nadále používá původní čtvercový obrázek.

Širší podklad byl vytvořen vestavěným nástrojem imagegen z `public/recepce-fotografie-2k.webp`. Generátor doplnil okolí recepce. Výstup 1448 × 1086 px byl exportován pomocí Sharp / Lanczos3 jako WebP 2560 × 1920 px; nejde o nativní fotografické rozlišení 2560 px.

Použitý prompt:

Edit the supplied dental reception photograph for a website background. Produce a photorealistic 4:3 landscape image at least 2560 pixels wide. OUTPAINT horizontally only: keep the original square photograph exactly in the central 75% of the new canvas width, extend each side by 1/6 of the original width. Preserve original pixels/composition in that central square as faithfully as possible: same gray rectangular reception counter, same two potted plants, exact hanging greenery, same lighting and wall textures, same camera angle. Preserve the physical raised white text on counter exactly: first line Z U B Y, second line D Á S N Ě, with accurate Czech accents and no other lettering. Natural restrained continuation of light gray wall on left, greenery and counter continuing on right, floor tiles continuing at bottom sides. No new furniture, no redesign, no blur strips or mirrored repeated edges. This is a wider framing of the SAME room, not a new room. Do not crop top or bottom of original square. Natural real architectural photography.
